Shenzhen · Summit ReportedXinhua · Kremlin

Three presidents meet — and sign a peace declaration

Trump, Xi and Putin talked for some 80 minutes at Kylin Villa, then issued a four-point ‘Declaration Toward Peace’, released by Xinhua and the Kremlin. It pledges dialogue on cutting nuclear risks, steadier fuel supplies and restraint by war-crimes courts. It names neither Ukraine nor Iran, and sets no timetable.

Worldwide · Games ReportedSensor Tower · Streams Charts

GTA VI lands to records: 17m copies, a 99 score

The sequel unlocked at midnight in New Zealand and rolled west all day. Sensor Tower estimates 17.1m copies and $1.65bn already — three-quarters of them on PlayStation — while critics average 99 and a record 7.9m people watched streams at once. Take-Two’s first official count is due Friday.

3
New York · Markets ReportedReuters · Bloomberg

Anthropic starts trading after the biggest float ever

The Claude maker’s shares opened on the Nasdaq after Wednesday’s $83 pricing raised $81.3bn. Side markets had run 6 to 39 per cent above the offer price, and one perpetual quoted $100.70 on Thursday morning. The closing price, set after this page, gives the true mark.

The Hague · Law ReportedICJ · Guardian

World Court: Myanmar did commit genocide

In 13 rulings, judges found genocide by 9 to 6 and failure to punish it by 11 to 4, counting some 10,000 killed in 2017. Return, restitution and compensation are ordered.

5
Antalya · Climate ReportedReuters

COP31’s new draft drops fossil fuels entirely

Thursday’s rewritten deal text, cut from 144 points to 80, drops every mention of ending fossil fuels. It still concedes warming may pass 1.5°C. One more revision is due before Friday’s close.

6
Odisha · Storm ForecastIMD · IANS

Akvan nears; 204,070 listed to evacuate

Odisha’s relief chief lists 204,070 people for possible evacuation; about 50,000 had already moved by Thursday. Schools in six districts shut for three days. Landfall is due Friday near Puri.

7
Crimea · War ReportedAP

Kerch Bridge hit a third time in four days

Neptune missiles struck the rail span overnight into Thursday, halting trains until about 16:00, Crimea’s Russian-installed head said. Ukraine’s defence ministry claimed the strike.

8
Gaza · Ceasefire ReportedReuters

Israel calls its Gaza tank raid finished

The army said Thursday the operation west of the Yellow Line, begun Wednesday near Bureij and Nuseirat against a suspected Hamas tunnel, is concluded. The Salah al-Din road has reopened.

9
Riyadh · Gulf ClaimReuters

Coalition: Houthi missile stopped over Riyadh

Sirens sounded before dawn and falling debris caused damage, the Saudi-led coalition said on Thursday. A Houthi claim to have hit the airport is unverified. Civil Defence later said the danger had passed.

10
Seoul · Education ReportedAJP

551,864 sit the exam that stops a nation

Candidates sat the CSAT at 1,312 centres from 08:40 to 17:45; offices opened at ten and jets were hushed for the listening test. A nationwide phone alert exposed 22 who had kept their phones.

11
Jakarta · Law ReportedIndonesiaNow

Indonesia passes its seize-first asset law

Parliament ended an 18-year wait at 09:30, letting the state seize suspect assets of Rp1bn or more before any conviction, by court order. A new independent body will manage what is taken.

12
Paris · Politics ReportedAFP

Greens swing to Mélenchon, and split the left

Marine Tondelier’s step toward the Mélenchon camp drew a Thursday protest from Raphaël Glucksmann. Socialist chief Olivier Faure concedes the momentum is real.

13
Bogotá · Labour ReportedEl Centavo

Unions hold a national strike in Colombia

The CUT and Fecode unions marched in Bogotá, Medellín and Cali against the ‘Economic Rescue’ tax bill. The strike held even after the government dropped the food-VAT element on Tuesday.

14
Canberra · Economy ReportedABS

Australia’s jobless rate climbs to 4.9%

The October figures, out Thursday, show a five-year high: employment fell 43,500 against forecasts of a 25,000 gain. Some 753,700 people are now out of work, the bureau counts.

15
Florida · Weather ReportedCNN

Floods close South Florida schools again

Up to 15 inches of rain fell on Hollywood in a day, with some 300 rescue calls in a night. Broward, Miami-Dade, Brevard and Volusia closed Thursday; Volusia stays shut Friday.
